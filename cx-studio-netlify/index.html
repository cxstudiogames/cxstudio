How it runs

Five stages, the same on every project, all done online. You always know what happens next.

01

Brief

A short call or chat. We learn your business, your customers and what the site has to achieve.

  • Video call
  • Goals
  • Your logo & photos
02

Design

A direction made only for you: colours, type, motion. You see it before a line of code is written.

  • Moodboard
  • Animated preview
03

Build

We build the site, the animations and any tools it needs, and test it on phones and desktops.

  • Mobile first
  • Speed checked
04

Launch

Domain, hosting, email and analytics connected. Your site goes live and starts working.

  • Domain & DNS
  • Go live
05

Care & grow

Monthly maintenance and, if you want, ads and social accounts run by us, with a report every month.

  • Ads
